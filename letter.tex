Dear Dr. Williams,

I am applying for the position of Executive Director, Assessment, Accountability, and Continuous Improvement. My work in educational leadership has consistently integrated the needs of our most at-risk students and the complex program and fiscal systems that align resources to meet those needs. Since my first Federal Program Monitoring review as a TOSA -- Compliance Coordinator through my current work synthesizing, analyzing, and visualizing student data across the county, I have focused on addressing opportunity gaps for students through shared stewardship with district and site leaders. This through-line would continue as Executive Director.

This position also requires the ability to lead in partnership with other agencies for student success, especially given RCOE's role as a GeoLead, supporting local districts and building capacity in peer COEs. In my time at RCOE, I have built relationships across the state through co-leading the Data Visualization Network, a statewide collaborative of COE data practitioners. My expertise in accountability and the associated data has also led to recurring partnership with the California Collaborative for Educational Excellence (CCEE), as exemplified in shared presentations with the CCEE and the California Department of Education, as well as with California School Information Services. Topics for these varied presentations centered around basics of the then-new California School Dashboard, crucial considerations for districts in selecting data products, along with methods for implementing a modern data stack within the bounds of K-12 data security and privacy practice.

These partnerships are a reflection of years of hands-on work in assessment and accountability. My experience as a TOSA -- Compliance Coordinator showed me the realities of addressing complex statutory requirements while building the capacity of principals in effective school planning. As a Curriculum and Instruction Coordinator in Menifee Union, I moved the assessment system forward through improved test practices, such as adoption of modern computerized infrastructure for GATE testing, while also improving the test instruments themselves, such as the statistical analysis of local test instruments for psychometric alignment with state testing. In Menifee, I also supported the authorship of the Local Control and Accountability Plan, as well as implementation monitoring systems for LCAP actions. Finally, at RCOE, I have had a dual focus on technical data systems integration, and the data governance needed to maintain reliable, secure, and trusted data partnerships with twenty-three districts. These data systems supported our districts in data quality, contributing to our leading graduation rate of 93.2\% among the largest counties, as well as the delivery of over 35,000 fee waivers through the CSU Direct Admissions Pilot over the last two years.

As a whole, these experiences prepare me with the breadth of background that our unit needs. AACI is composed of capable, experienced leaders; accordingly, our unit needs someone with the judgment to validate when we're moving in the right direction, and to provide course correction when we are missing the bigger picture --- whether that's the RCOE organizational landscape, the local realities of each of our districts, or the state and federal policy context. I can provide this guidance, while strengthening our partnerships with districts and external relationships across the System of Support as the next Executive Director.

\vspace{2mm}
Thank you,
