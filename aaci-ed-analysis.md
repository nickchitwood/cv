## 1. Essential Functions (from JD, paraphrased)

The JD marks 11 functions as Essential (E). Function 12 ("perform work safely") is boilerplate and excluded.

1. **Directs, plans, coordinates, supervises** assessment and accountability activities to ensure an effective countywide program.
2. **Explains State and Federal accountability systems** and measures to stakeholders.
3. **Leadership to plan, design, and deliver centralized and customized services** that support **balanced assessment systems**.
4. **Direction and support to districts** with annual review, update, and development of **LCAPs** (compliance).
5. **Leads team in conducting review of all district LCAPs** and **provides Superintendent with data and information for plan approvals**.
6. **Collects and analyzes data** for reporting findings to stakeholders via written documentation and oral presentations.
7. **Develops mechanisms to measure impact** of services/supports on **student achievement, teacher practice, instructional leadership, and district systems of support**.
8. **County-wide networking of school districts** to exchange strategies and manage data related to standards, achievement, and accountability reporting.
9. **Receives and responds to inquiries** relating to program evaluation and educational research.
10. **Leads staff to provide technical expertise to district officials** in assessment construction/validation, **psychometric analyses**, and data interpretation.
11. **Participates in the selection, evaluation, training, and supervision of staff** to ensure effective program outcomes.

### Non-E functions worth tracking

- **California System of Support** participation, including **geographic lead agency** and **subject matter lead** initiatives.
- **Differentiated Assistance** records and county office collaboration.
- **Annual preliminary budget** for assigned programs and projects.
- **Smarter Balanced + Common Core State Standards** knowledge (named explicitly).
- **Grant and special program management**.

### Minimum Qualifications (gates)

- 5+ years assessment & accountability — **met**.
- 5+ years administrative experience in a public school setting **including site-level principal AND district-level administrator** — **partial** (district-level: yes via Menifee C&I Coordinator + RUSD TOSA-Compliance Coordinator; **site-level principal: gap**).
- Master's required, doctorate preferred — **met** (Ed.D. UCLA, 2018).
- California Teaching Credential + Clear Administrative Credential — **met**.

## 4. Reorder proposal for AACI Administrator bullets (CV)

Current CV order leads with 8 consecutive data-program bullets (CalKIDS, CSU Direct, SBHIP, data warehouse, DSAs, Superintendent Initiatives data support, COE Network, Student Data PLC), with accountability/CI/LCAP work in positions 9–14. For a role titled *Assessment, Accountability, and Continuous Improvement*, scanning the first few bullets reads as "data, data, data, data" — positioning for a data-team-lead, not for the ED.

Proposed reorder (no content cuts — just sequence):

1. LCAP Fastpass team (accountability, Superintendent-facing).
2. SoCal SOS Continuous Improvement Data Subteam (names "Continuous Improvement" verbatim).
3. Co-facilitated RCAN (assessment leadership, county network).
4. Statewide Data Visualization COE Network co-leadership (statewide).
5. Cross-divisional Student Data PLC (internal coordination / governance).
6. Program evaluations (EF 9).
7. Data warehouse architecture with EF7 framing.
8. CalKIDS data operations (flagship program).
9. CSU Direct Admissions data systems.
10. SBHIP data support.
11. Superintendent Initiatives data/evaluation support.
12. Data privacy / DSA negotiations.
13. Peer technical leadership / 48% project-volume contribution.
14. Evaluator of record for AACI administrative support.
15. Classified Administrator of the Year (2024).

Accountability, continuous improvement, and statewide leadership come first; major data programs become *evidence of how* the work happened, not the work itself.
